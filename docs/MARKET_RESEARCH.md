# Market Research Guide for Balorg AI Pricing

## Overview
This document provides a framework for conducting comprehensive market research to validate and optimize the Balorg AI pricing strategy.

## Research Objectives

### Primary Goals
1. Validate proposed pricing tiers and amounts
2. Understand willingness to pay by customer segment
3. Identify competitive positioning opportunities
4. Determine optimal discount structures
5. Understand customer decision-making factors

### Key Questions to Answer
- What price points maximize revenue while ensuring accessibility?
- How do our prices compare to competitors?
- What features are most valuable to each customer segment?
- What discount programs have the highest impact?
- What are the primary objections to our pricing?

### 2. Customer Surveys

#### Target Audience Segments
1. **Students & Academics**

2. **Individual Developers**

3. **Small Teams (2-10 people)**

4. **Medium Teams (11-50 people)**
   - Growing companies
   - Established businesses
   - Tech companies

5. **Enterprise (50+ people)**
   - Large corporations
   - Government agencies
   - Multi-national organizations

#### Survey Questions Template

**Demographic Questions:**
1. What is your role?
2. What is your organization size?
3. What industry are you in?
4. What is your location?

**Product Usage Questions:**
5. How would you use Balorg AI?
6. How many API calls per month do you anticipate?
7. What features are most important to you?
8. What alternatives are you currently using?

**Pricing Sensitivity Questions:**
9. What would you expect to pay for this service monthly?
   - [ ] $0 (Free only)
   - [ ] $1-10
   - [ ] $11-25
   - [ ] $26-50
   - [ ] $51-100
   - [ ] $101-200
   - [ ] $200+
   - [ ] Custom enterprise pricing

10. At what price point would this be:
    - Too expensive (won't consider)?
    - Expensive but would consider?
    - Good value?
    - Too cheap (suspicious of quality)?

**Feature Value Questions:**
11. Rank these features by importance (1-10):
    - API access
    - Advanced AI models
    - Priority support
    - Team collaboration
    - Custom fine-tuning
    - Analytics dashboard
    - On-premise deployment

**Discount Program Questions:**
12. Which discount programs are relevant to you?
13. What discount percentage would make you more likely to subscribe?
14. Would you commit to an annual plan for a discount?

### 3. User Interviews

#### Interview Guide

**Opening (5 minutes)**
- Build rapport
- Explain purpose
- Get permission to record

**Current Situation (10 minutes)**
- What AI tools do you currently use?
- What problems are you trying to solve?
- What is your budget for AI tools?
- Who makes purchasing decisions?

**Product Reaction (15 minutes)**
- Show pricing page
- What is your first impression?
- Which plan would you choose and why?
- What concerns do you have?
- What's missing?

**Price Testing (15 minutes)**
- Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter:
  1. At what price would it be so expensive you wouldn't consider it?
  2. At what price would it be expensive but you'd consider it?
  3. At what price would it be a bargain?
  4. At what price would it be so cheap you'd question the quality?

**Closing (5 minutes)**
- Any other feedback?
- Would you be interested in early access?
- Can we follow up?

#### Target Sample Size
- 20-30 interviews per customer segment
- Mix of current users and prospects
- Include churned users for insights

### 4. A/B Testing Plan

#### Test Variations

**Test 1: Price Points**
- Variant A: Current pricing ($19, $99)
- Variant B: Lower entry ($15, $79)
- Variant C: Higher value ($19, $129)

**Test 2: Billing Cycle Emphasis**
- Variant A: Default to monthly
- Variant B: Default to annual (with savings highlighted)
- Variant C: Side-by-side comparison

**Test 3: Discount Prominence**
- Variant A: Discounts below pricing
- Variant B: Discounts integrated into pricing cards
- Variant C: Discount banner at top

**Test 4: Feature Presentation**
- Variant A: Simple bullet list
- Variant B: Detailed feature descriptions
- Variant C: Feature comparison table

#### Success Metrics
- Conversion rate (visitor to trial)
- Plan selection distribution
- Time to decision
- Bounce rate
- Trial to paid conversion
- Average revenue per user

## Analysis Framework

### Price Optimization Models

#### Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter
Plot four price points from surveys:
1. Too expensive
2. Expensive
3. Good value
4. Too cheap

Find optimal price range at intersections.

#### Gabor-Granger Technique
Present different prices and measure:
- Purchase intent at each price
- Demand curve
- Revenue optimization point

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Analysis

```
LTV = (Average Revenue per Customer) × (Customer Lifespan in Months) × (Profit Margin)
```

**Target LTV/CAC Ratio:** 3:1 or higher

### Market Sizing

**TAM (Total Addressable Market)**
- Global AI/ML market size
- Relevant segments
- Annual growth rate

**SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)**
- Markets we can realistically serve
- Geographic limitations
- Regulatory considerations

**SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)**
- Realistic market share in 1-3 years
- Based on competitive positioning
- Sales and marketing capacity
