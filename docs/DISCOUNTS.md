# Balorg AI Discount Programs

## Overview
Balorg AI is committed to making advanced AI technology accessible to everyone. Our discount programs are designed to support students, non-profits, early adopters, and users with different financial situations.

## Available Discount Programs

### 1. 🎓 Student Discount Program

**Discount:** 50% off any paid plan

#### Eligibility
- Currently enrolled students at accredited educational institutions
- Valid student status (undergraduate, graduate, or PhD)
- Must be 13 years or older

#### How to Apply
1. Sign up for a Balorg AI account
2. Navigate to Settings > Billing > Apply Discount
3. Select "Student Discount"
4. Provide one of the following:
   - Valid .edu email address (instant approval)
   - Student ID document upload
   - Enrollment verification letter

#### Verification Process
- **.edu email:** Instant automatic approval
- **Document upload:** Reviewed within 24-48 hours
- **Annual reverification:** Required every 12 months

#### Terms
- Discount applies for duration of student status
- Cannot be combined with other discounts (except referral credits)
- Must reverify annually
- Discount ends 30 days after graduation or loss of student status

### 5. 🌍 Income-Based Discount Program

**Discount:** Variable (10%-60% based on financial situation)

#### Philosophy
We believe AI should be accessible regardless of financial circumstances. This program offers sliding scale pricing based on income and geographic location.

#### Eligibility

- Users in developing countries
- Those experiencing financial hardship

#### How to Apply

- Confidential review by dedicated team
- Review completed within 5-7 business days
- All information kept strictly confidential
- No discrimination based on application

- **Tier 3 (30% off):** Moderate income or developing country

Additional considerations for users in:
- Low-income countries (per World Bank classification)
- High cost-of-living areas with lower local wages
- Countries with currency devaluation

#### Terms
- Discount valid for 6-12 months depending on tier
- Reverification required at renewal
- All information strictly confidential
- Cannot be combined with other discounts
- May be combined with referral credits

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I upgrade plans while keeping my discount?
Yes! Your discount follows you when you upgrade. For example, a student with 50% off Starter can upgrade to Professional and maintain the 50% discount.

### What happens if my discount expires mid-billing cycle?
You'll receive 30 days notice. If not renewed, the standard pricing applies at your next billing cycle, not immediately.

### Can non-profits get bulk discounts too?
Yes! Non-profits receive 40% off, and bulk discounts can potentially be negotiated for very large teams. Contact sales for custom quotes.

### How long does verification take?
- Instant: .edu email verification, referral credits
- 24-48 hours: Student ID verification
- 3-5 days: Non-profit verification
- 5-7 days: Income-based verification

### Is my information kept confidential?
Absolutely. All verification documents are:
- Encrypted in transit and at rest
- Accessed only by verification team
- Deleted after verification period
- Never sold or shared with third parties

### Can I apply for multiple discount programs?
You can apply, but only one percentage-based discount can be active at a time. We'll apply whichever gives you the best savings.
